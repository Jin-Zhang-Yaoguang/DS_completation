This exchange really captures the current sentiment on Kaggle. The shift from manual feature crafting to multi-agent LLM orchestration is undeniable, and the compute/budget divide is real. However, there is still hope for self-taught practitioners and students: LLMs can generate code, but they cannot design a leak-free validation strategy.
Without rock-solid local CV discipline and a deep theoretical grasp of the problem, orchestrating 20 models will just produce 20 ways to overfit the public leaderboard. The "John Connors" of data science won't be the ones with the largest API budget—they'll be the ones who understand the data deeply enough to direct the machines effectively. Keep grinding the fundamentals; tools change every 6 months, but core statistical intuition and rigorous validation never expire.

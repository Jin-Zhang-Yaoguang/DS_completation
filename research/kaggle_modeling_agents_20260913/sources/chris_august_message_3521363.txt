Human data science vs. a 150-LLM compute factory
Honestly? Congrats on the win, but this writeup leaves a pretty bitter taste…
As a solo competitor balancing an 8-hour daily job, a real life, and running code on free Kaggle notebooks and Colab tiers in my spare time, seeing 1st place bagged by an automated swarm of 150 LLMs running hundreds of models on massive GPU power feels completely ridiculous.
While many of us spent weeks actually doing data science — analyzing features by hand, tuning target encodings, and crafting ensembles line by line — this was basically a flex of who has the biggest API budget and compute cluster. Is Kaggle still about modeling skills, or are we just benchmarking corporate GPU wallets now?
I finished in the Top 1000 with a 0.96907 private score — literally 0.002 away from the winning multi-agent swarm. That 0.002 gap proves that human intuition and solid CV setups can still hold their ground against an AI factory, but the playing field is clearly broken.
Respect for the agent engineering, but to everyone who built their pipeline from scratch after a long workday: hold your head high. We did actual data science here..
How do you all feel about the future of Kaggle in this new agentic era? Id love to hear honest thoughts from fellow solo competitors on where we draw the line between fair learning and brute-force compute.
